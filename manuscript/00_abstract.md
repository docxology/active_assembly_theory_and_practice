# Abstract {#sec:abstract}

Assembly traces can support explicit probabilistic inference without being
treated as recovered program meaning. `{{PROJECT_TITLE}}` makes that distinction
executable: transparent x86-64 fixtures are decoded with Capstone, traced under
bounded Unicorn emulation, and mapped to typed observations that distinguish
missing, unseen, and unsupported evidence. Two Generalized Notation Notation
(GNN) specifications define passive-phase and prospective sensor models whose
tensors drive real pymdp execution. A native categorical Julia/RxInfer companion
consumes the passive model's same parameters [@friedman2026gnn; @heins2022pymdp;
@bagaev2023rxinfer]. The sensor Agent selects an action before fixture evidence
is acquired. Independent finite calculations check beliefs and policy scores;
native smoothing uses the same parameters and first-observation convention.
Every inference comparator and fitted diagnostic also consumes an
upstream-parsed numeric GNN specification, with source-bound runtime instances
and rendered model graphs.

A corpus of `{{TEMPORAL_FIXTURE_COUNT}}` authored fixtures supports
complete-fixture exclusion diagnostics. At the fixed informative likelihood,
the memoryless decoder outperforms the temporal filter; temporal advantages
appear under synthetic observation ambiguity. Designed category ambiguity
remains a visible failure case. The separate Gaussian and categorical Bethe
free-energy diagnostics are not compared numerically with policy expected
free energy.

The quotient benchmark contains
`{{QUOTIENT_CLASSIFIER_FAMILY_COUNT}}` transformation-family identifiers and
`{{QUOTIENT_CLASSIFIER_ROW_COUNT}}` rows, with
`{{QUOTIENT_CLASSIFIER_HELD_OUT_FAMILY_COUNT}}` families held out from model fitting.
The co-primary portfolio includes an interpretable classifier, a temporal
probabilistic model, a fixed one-layer tanh recurrent classifier, and surface
baselines;
the temporal endpoint reaches balanced accuracy
`{{QUOTIENT_CLASSIFIER_TEMPORAL_BALANCED_ACCURACY}}` and macro-F1
`{{QUOTIENT_CLASSIFIER_TEMPORAL_MACRO_F1}}` on this frozen bounded benchmark.
Held-out identifiers share authored template lineages with the fitting data.
Family-resampling intervals and conditional randomization diagnostics therefore
describe this finite inventory; the retrospective protocol correction withholds
superiority claims.
A complementary pinned static-only corpus supplies `{{EXTERNAL_LABEL_PROJECT_COUNT}}`
licensed source projects and `{{EXTERNAL_LABEL_ROW_COUNT}}` source-contract rows,
including `{{EXTERNAL_LABEL_EQUIVALENT_COUNT}}` lexical witnesses,
`{{EXTERNAL_LABEL_NEAR_MISS_COUNT}}` contract-directed near misses, and
`{{EXTERNAL_LABEL_NEGATIVE_COUNT}}` identity controls. The corpus establishes
provenance and coverage; it does not execute arbitrary external binaries or support
application-effectiveness claims.

Selected normalized machine-state relations are checked by Python, native
execution, and a bounded Lean fragment. Exact finite sensing certificates and
source-bound reader artifacts make assumptions and failures inspectable.
Independent physical replication remains unearned. The contribution is a
reproducible substrate for testing assembly-to-inference mechanisms, with
explicit limits on semantics, statistical generalization, and external validity.
