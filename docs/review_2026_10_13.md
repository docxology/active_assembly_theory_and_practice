# Correctness and methods review — 2026-10-13

The review began from a clean `main` synchronized with the private upstream.
It covered the observation and inference code, finite benchmark statistics,
provenance and cache boundaries, registered figures, bibliography and citation
roles, manuscript claims, documentation, and the reproducibility envelope.
It is a local engineering and scientific-methods review, not peer review,
independent replay, independent reimplementation, or proof of complete ISA
semantics. Each demonstrated defect was repaired with a bounded regression or
the existing source-bound validation gate.

## Protocol and scientific interpretation

Protocol 2.2 records the retrospective correction. Quotient labels, the family
assignment, co-primary metric definitions and practical margin are retained.
Training-only vocabularies and corrected numerical calculations may change
reported values; outputs must be regenerated rather than edited to preserve
older numbers. No superiority claim is promoted from this correction. A new
inferential comparison must be prospectively frozen and independently evaluated.

The temporal corpus's legacy `source_program_family` key means fixture identity.
Related authored fixtures are dependent. The current leave-one-fixture-out
estimators exclude their scored fixture but can train across the declared
inventory partitions. They do not establish lineage-disjoint generalization.

External source acquisition remains pinned, licensed and static-only. All ten
archive/license rows were reacquired and validated locally during this review.
That does not satisfy the independently replayed external recipe gate. The
independent physical-machine replay and reimplementation gates remain open.
